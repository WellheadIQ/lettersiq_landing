# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are Texas oil & gas operators — often the operations or compliance lead inside a company — who need to keep every lease they run out of severance and shut-in trouble. They work under time pressure and can't afford to learn about a problem from the post office. A secondary audience of landmen and regulatory specialists supports multiple operators or larger portfolios and leans on the same signal across many leases at once.

## Product Purpose

LettersIQ warns operators early enough to cure a problem before a severance or shut-in ever lands. It watches the upstream Texas Railroad Commission signals that precede an enforcement action and turns them into a daily briefing that keeps users ahead of trouble. Success is twofold: no operator is ever surprised by a severance they could have prevented, and every morning starts with a clear, ranked list of what needs attention and what to do next.

## Positioning

Protect the entire operating portfolio — including the neighboring leases on your shared commingles. LettersIQ surfaces the blast radius that never appears in your own filings, because the triggering violation was filed against someone else.

## Brand Personality

Precise, urgent, operational, and authoritative. The product should read like control-room tooling: dense signal with unmistakable priority, plain language that earns trust over decorative chrome, and just enough of the Lone Star instrument-panel character to feel specific to Texas oil & gas — without tipping into costume. It speaks like an expert colleague who has already done the query for you.

## Anti-references

Not generic SaaS: no purple gradients, endless identical feature-card grids, or interchangeable "game-changer" testimonials. Not consumer fintech or lifestyle softness: no playful, rounded, emoji-forward tone. And not oilfield kitsch or fake-terminal cosplay: no derrick clipart, cowboy motifs, or decorative command-line theater standing in for real product substance.

## Design Principles

Blast radius first — lead with the risk that doesn't show up in a user's own records. Early signal over late mail — upstream RRC cues always beat waiting on a certified letter. Morning to next cure — every view should end in a clear, specific action, not just a status. Authority without costume — carry credibility through operational density and plain language, not thematic decoration. Show the artifact — real alerts, briefings, and dashboards over abstract feature claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across the product: verified contrast on text and UI, full keyboard operability, visible focus, and meaningful labels. Honor reduced-motion preferences with non-motion alternatives, and never rely on color alone to convey severity or state.
